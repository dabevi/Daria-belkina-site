Elsewhere Gallery, 2025

Daria Belkina · single-channel video (loop), 01:00 · sound

The gallery as a threshold rather than a destination. The camera moves continuously through the paintings, joining spaces into a seamless loop with no entrance and no exit — a passage that never resolves into arrival.

You are standing in front of a printed mark. The work itself is not here: it exists only as a digital version, reached by crossing over — the same crossing the video never stops performing.

Daria Belkina · contemporary artist dariabelkina.art
