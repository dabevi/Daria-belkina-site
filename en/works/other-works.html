Repeat Without Return, 2026

Photographic series, 10 images (3 shown below)
Digital photography, no digital intervention
Digital format only — physical exhibition format not yet defined

This series is constructed as a sequence of images that are perceived as the same place, although each was taken at a different point along an urban route. The photographs document different staircases along an embankment, but the repetition of angle, distance and composition creates the impression that the viewer is encountering the same fragment of space again and again. What emerges is an experience of repetition without coincidence.

Read more

The staircase traditionally connotes transition, movement, the possibility of changing level or state. In this series it retains the form of a promise but loses the function of a completed passage. Each frame reads as an attempted step that leads to no exit. Space does not open — it closes in.

The repetition here is neither decorative nor typological. It operates as a mechanism of cognitive entrapment: the viewer is certain of having seen this place before but cannot prove the match. Memory begins to substitute for fact. Distinct points of the city accumulate into the experience of a single step that cannot be completed.

The series was made during a temporary disruption of the habitual spatial order. Access to these viewpoints was possible only for a brief period — when the surface of the water became a solid ground and allowed a position that is ordinarily inaccessible. This temporary transformation of the environment does not become the subject of the work, but it determines its logic: transition exists only as a brief possibility that then disappears.

There is no digital intervention, artificial distortion or visual glitch effect. The disruption arises within perception itself. Different places look the same. Space simulates familiarity. The route promises progress but each time returns to the starting point.

Render Error: Reality, 2025

Single-channel video, 3:27
1904 × 1088, 24 fps, colour, sound (stereo)
Official selection, Artefact Short Film Festival 2025

Frames from video. Watch on Google Drive

Render Error: Reality investigates human perception at a moment when reality is increasingly experienced through the screen as an intermediary. The screen in this work functions not as a neutral window but as an active mechanism of selection, filtering and construction of the visible — a space in which the sense of stability is constantly undermined.

Read more

The screen world here is not an alternative to reality but its managed surface: a visual layer concealing zones of error, malfunction and incompletion. The screen window becomes a frame separating "here" from "there," defining the limits of what we are able to see and what we are permitted to see. The screen ceases to be a portal to a "better life" and is revealed as a mechanism for forming the visible.

The glitch in the work is not an effect or a breakdown but becomes a moment in which the process of image formation itself becomes visible — a point at which control weakens and tension becomes perceptible.

The work does not oppose the digital and the material but investigates the state of ambivalence that arises between them as a result of mounting anxiety. This ambivalence forms a closed cycle in which each constantly generates the other. Digital space in the work exists as a domain of continuous morphogenesis: never stable, constantly assembling itself from noise, light and algorithmic conjecture.

The world in Render Error: Reality thus appears as a continuous process of becoming and loss — reflecting the experience of existential anxiety connected to the impossibility of stopping, of making a final choice, of fixing a point of support.
